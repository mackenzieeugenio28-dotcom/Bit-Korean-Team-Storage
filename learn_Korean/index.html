How it works

One drama line, four tiny steps

Every lesson follows the same loop, so you always know what is next. A full lesson takes about 5 minutes.

  1. 1

    Listen & guess

    Hear the line (via romanisation) and guess what it means before you peek.

  2. 2

    Line breakdown

    See the Korean script and every word explained piece by piece.

  3. 3

    Words & grammar

    Save the key vocabulary and learn the one grammar point that makes the line tick.

  4. 4

    Mini quiz

    Answer three questions. Finish the lesson and earn 10 XP plus a streak day.
